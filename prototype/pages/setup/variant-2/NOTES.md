# The Circuit

## The bet
What must be true is a path, so draw it: agents, breaker, engine, mandate check, keychain, PayPal sandbox, with HOUSE as counterparty. A missing piece is a red break in the wire, counted in the headline. A gold bracket marks what only Maya can set, in the approval window, and locks visibly after 15 idle minutes. Pause is a breaker.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the headline, the compact circuit, and a "Breaks" group: one row per break with its default on silence and the one act that closes it (Probe, Store… ↗, Sign… ↗).
2. Click a node or use ←/→; the inspector fills with the part's state, controls, who may set it and "silent → …".
3. Layer 2: "Details…" opens the sheet with the long story (probe, keychain, mandates, agents, HOUSE, idle lock); PayPal's opens the three money authorities.
4. Flip the breaker (or P) to pause every agent.

## What it does better than the other two
It shows cause and effect: stored credentials sit dark behind a missing mandate. Only this variant draws the money authorities and the idle-lock boundary on the picture, and every break is fixable without leaving the screen.

## Known limits
- At 1024 wide node labels hide and values truncate.
- Inspector and breaks list are two places to act.
- No autostart toggle (it does not gate money).
- Pointer-first; the keyboard covers selection and the breaker.
