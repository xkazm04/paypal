# Counter board

## The bet
Everything on one sheet, in the order the question asks it. Live quotes come first, one line each. Below them the catalog is a dense ledger with a floor stepper on every row. A bottom bar gathers floor edits into one S-2 draft that only the approval window can sign. It is the built baseline, sharpened to fit 1280×800.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: scan the seats (who, item, state, countdown, amount, default on silence) and the ledger (list, floor, vs market, counter, feed).
2. Change floors with −/+, ↑/↓ (Shift ±$10) or by typing. The row turns gold and the bar lists the change. "1 quote withdrawn ›" shows the effect.
3. Layer 2: click a seat or row to fill the inspector with the quote's stages, timeline and words, or the item's scale, market, description and stock. "note ›" opens untrusted text, "fix ›" the feed export.
4. "Review & sign in approval ↗" (Ctrl+Enter) opens the approval window.

## What it does better than the other two
It shows the most at once: the fastest way to compare and edit many floors, prices and feed states.

## Known limits
- At 1280 wide, the inspector folds the module list to dots.
- Floor vs market is short text per row. The visual scale is only in the inspector.
- List prices are read-only.
