# Price rail

## The bet
"Are my floors right?" is a spatial question, so the page is one shared axis. Each item's lane is drawn as a share of its list price. The floor is a handle you drag, the market is a band with a ◆ median, and quotes are pins. The people at the counter sit in the right column.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: scan the rail and its fact chips. The visitors column shows who, item, amount, state and when each quote lapses.
2. Drag a handle, or focus it and use ←/→ (Shift ±$10, Delete reverts), or type the floor. The draft pill lists moved floors and the default on silence.
3. Click a visitor to find its lane. Layer 2: an item title (or Enter on a handle) opens the item sheet, and a pin or "›" opens the quote sheet. Notes, the legend and the draft's impact open as popovers.
4. "Review & sign in approval ↗" opens the approval window.

## What it does better than the other two
It shows every floor against the market and live quotes at once, and moving a floor is a drag.

## Known limits
- The axis covers 60–110% of list. A lower floor shows ◂ and must be typed.
- An item with no market reference shows only its floor.
- Exact figures live in the floor field and the sheets.
