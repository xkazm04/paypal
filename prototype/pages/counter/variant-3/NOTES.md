# Journal and floor walk

## The bet
Two stories side by side. On the left, the counter reads as a transcript, one line per step, tagged by who acted, so Maya sees why no sale needs her. On the right, the floors are a keyboard-first walk, one item per card, the most urgent first. A review hands off to the approval window.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: read lark's thread ($61 quoted, $58 floor held) and fern-ops' thread (their note passed as data, the order waiting on the buyer). Their words sit behind "note ›".
2. Layer 2: a thread's top row opens the whole conversation in a sheet, with the untrusted note quarantined in its bubble.
3. In the walk, ←/→ moves the floor (Shift ±$10), Enter keeps it, J/K step and R reverts. "Facts & catalog ›" opens the item sheet. A thread's "Check the … floor →" jumps to its item.
4. S opens the review (diff, impact, default on silence). S again opens the approval window. E exports the feed.

## What it does better than the other two
It makes the trust story legible (who said what, who acted), and floors get a complete pass.

## Known limits
- One item at a time is slower for bulk changes.
- The walk order is fixed at page load.
- Steps are cut to one line. The full text is in the thread sheet.
