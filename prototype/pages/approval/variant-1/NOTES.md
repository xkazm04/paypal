# The Card

## The bet
Everything Rust checked fits on one small face, so Maya answers "is this what I signed?" in one glance and acts at the foot. It's the built `DealReview`, sharpened, with the clock, the default and the buttons always in the footer.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the 26px amount with a state chip (READY · 3/7), the sentence "Pay Dan (north-desk) for …", six ✓ rows, a market line, the countdown, "If you do nothing →", and Withdraw beside one gold button.
2. Click a check to see both sides and its source in a popover. ⓘ opens the market track, and the state chip opens the history.
3. **Details** (title bar) opens a sheet with every source, the market, the state history and the wallet log.
4. Press "Open PayPal in your browser". The checks fold to "6/6 ✓ at the moment you decided ›", and a one-line status and the poll take their place.

## What it does better than the other two
It's the fastest for routine approvals: one face reads the same for every decision kind. It's also the closest to the build, so the cheapest to ship.

## Known limits
- A glance can turn into a rubber stamp. Nothing slows a large amount down.
- A ✓ asserts a match, and both values are one click away.
- The percentile is interpolated (p65).
- Hello, polling and PayPal are simulated.
