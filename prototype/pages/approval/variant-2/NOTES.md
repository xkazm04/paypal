# Read-back

## The bet
Releasing money is rare, so it should be read, not glanced at. Like a pilot's read-back, it shows two facts per page. The money button exists only on page 4, which opens after pages 1–3 have each been shown once.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1 on each page: a four-step stepper, the title and two fact rows (value, "matches" chip, "against …"). Only the amount is 26px. "from ›" opens the source in a popover.
2. Walk with →/← or 1–4.
3. Page 4, Decide: the amount, six recap chips, the clock, "If you do nothing →" and one gold button. On a mismatch it's "Stopped", $339 ≠ $329, with no button.
4. After the hand-off, one status page carries IN BROWSER → APPROVED (polled) → RECEIPTED. **Details** opens the sources, the state history and the wallet log in a sheet. Withdraw stays in the footer.

## What it does better than the other two
It's the strongest guard against approving unseen, and it's keyboard-first: arrows walk the pages, and Enter does nothing.

## Known limits
- It's the slowest: three page turns before any decision, even for $9.60.
- Provenance is one click away (from ›), not printed on the page.
- Seeing a page isn't reading it. It's a speed bump, not proof of attention.
- Every decision kind is forced into four pages, and some pairs are thin.
