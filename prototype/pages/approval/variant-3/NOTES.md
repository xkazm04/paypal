# The Diff

## The bet
"Is this exactly what I signed?" is a comparison, so the window shows the comparison, not a ✓. Your side (teal) and the asked side (gold) sit in two columns, and a gutter names the relation (= same, ≤ within, ≠ differs). After the hand-off, the same table compares what you approved with what PayPal and the seller report.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the sentence, a twin header (on a mismatch, a red ≠ beside $339.00), six one-line diff rows, the market line, the countdown, "If you do nothing →" and the buttons.
2. Scan the gutter. A red row means no PayPal button. Click a row (or ↑/↓ and Space) to see both full values and the source in a popover.
3. After "Open PayPal in your browser", a second table, "You approved vs PayPal and Dan report", fills in as polls answer. The first table folds to one line.
4. **Details** opens the sources, the evidence, the state history and the wallet log in a sheet.

## What it does better than the other two
A mismatch is visible without reading a sentence. It's also the only variant that treats evidence after approval the same way as the checks before it.

## Known limits
- It's the widest, and long values cut off on layer 1.
- The lever's "policy vs proposal" columns read less naturally.
- The gutter vocabulary has to be learned once.
