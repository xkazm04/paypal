# Honor Clock

## The bet
This page's decision is about time: a hold running out its 72-hour honor period. The hold is drawn as a clock from authorized Thu 09:02 to auto-void Sun 09:02. A handle answers "what if I wait until…?"; waiting never moves money. Below it, the week is a calendar: each ask on its day, each day against the clause-5 cap.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1, the hold card: $64.00 as the one key figure, "silent → auto-void Sun 09:02 · nothing is paid", the track and a one-line readout.
2. Drag the handle, or use the arrow keys or Now / +24 h / +48 h / Deadline. It reads "still held · nothing moves" until the deadline, where "the policy voids the hold".
3. Layer 2 on demand: ⓘ gives the clause-7 reason, "note ›" the quarantined title, "Details" the evidence, ids and trace. Void opens a sheet with its consequences.
4. The week shows the refused GPUs ("Why refused? ›"), the policy captures, and the hold's band across Thu–Sun. A card opens its deal sheet.

## What it does better than the other two
It makes "silence never moves money" visible and testable. Only this variant shows velocity per day.

## Known limits
- The data has no daily engine spend, so engine spend is one number.
- At 1024 px, card times truncate and ids hide.
- One open hold only.
