# The Gate

## The bet
A firewall is a place, so draw it. Each ask is a lane running left to right through the M-12 clauses toward PayPal, and where its line stops is why. The GPUs stop at clause 3 and never reach PayPal. Policy captures turn green at clause 6. The dock turns gold at clause 7 and ends in a HELD capsule.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the "Needs you" row (ring, $64 held, "silent → auto-void Sun 09:02 · nothing is paid", Void, Review & capture ↗), then the matrix, which carries markers only.
2. Click "✗ refused". The inspector answers "Why refused?" with the clause, the verbatim refusal and 0 PayPal calls.
3. Click a clause header. The inspector lists every lane that clause stopped, routed or passed.
4. Click a lane or the capsule for its trace, PayPal ids and statement. "Details" and "All clauses" open sheets. "note ›" and the legend are popovers.
5. Void opens a sheet stating what it does. Capture hands off to the approval window.

## What it does better than the other two
It explains the firewall itself. Maya sees which clause does the work and that nothing refused touched PayPal.

## Known limits
- The inspector folds the sidebar below 1440 px. At 1024 px, lane names truncate.
- Lanes stay readable for about a dozen asks.
- Columns follow clause numbers, not Rust's check order.
