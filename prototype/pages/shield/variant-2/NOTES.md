# Evidence matrix

## The bet
The evidence grid comes first: each check is a row, each person who sat down is a column, and the conclusion rows (verdict, PayPal, your move) sit on top. Across a row you see why deal-hub-22 is final and pixel-bay releasable; a hatched "skipped, not passed" cell is hard to miss. Keyboard-first, with the inspector explaining whichever cell has focus.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: one short line per cell: HOLD / BLOCK / CLEAR, "0 calls", the check results, a "note ›" chip per counterparty. "Your move" holds the decision and "silent → lapses 20:00 · nothing is paid".
2. Layer 2: arrow into a cell and the inspector shows the rule, numbers, market band, matched phrase in the quarantined note, or the act in exact words.
3. `R` hands off to the approval window, where she types `pixel-bay` and releases. `K` keeps it held; "Refuse…" confirms in a sheet.
4. `P` ("What decided") dims passing checks; `O` opens the deal; Legend is a popover.

## What it does better than the other two
The same rules applied to everyone, nothing left out. With no engine, a whole row turns to "skipped · no engine" while every verdict stays put.

## Known limits
- Fits a few columns; a busy week needs paging.
- New counterparty is shown as HOLD (fixtures, §10.5); the §7 table says ASK.
- The approval window is simulated as an overlay.
