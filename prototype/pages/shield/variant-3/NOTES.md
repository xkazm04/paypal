# Gate replay

## The bet
Each intent walks through the shield's gate, one at a time: they asked → rules → market → text check → verdict → money. The token stops at the gate, and the last station carries the hand-off path from here to the approval window to the browser. It explains why, in order, instead of listing evidence.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: three seats (pixel-bay needs you, deal-hub-22, Dan), "0 PayPal calls while stopped", six stations with one short result each, the hand-off path, and the decision with "silent → lapses 20:00 · nothing is paid".
2. The gate replays pixel-bay on arrival; `Space` replays, `←` `→` step.
3. Layer 2: the selected station fills the inspector in plain words with its evidence (rules, market band, quarantined note, PayPal state).
4. Release in the approval window (type `pixel-bay` there), keep it held, or "Refuse…" (confirm sheet). The token then passes the gate.
5. `1`–`3` switch seats; deal-hub-22's gate is closed for good.

## What it does better than the other two
It teaches the order and the hand-off: "rules first, the engine only adds caution, money only past the approval window" lands in one replay.

## Known limits
- One intent at a time; comparing takes clicks.
- Auto-replay is skipped with reduced motion.
- New counterparty is shown as HOLD (fixtures, §10.5); the §7 table says ASK.
- The approval window is simulated as an overlay.
