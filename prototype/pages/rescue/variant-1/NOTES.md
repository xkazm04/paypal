# Lever Desk

## The bet
The sharpened baseline: renewals by PayPal's next retry, and beside them a desk for one subscriber. The bet is that Rescue is a short, calm list that goes fastest from the keyboard, with one subscriber's whole story one keypress away.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1, the list: one two-line row per renewal (id · plan · lever · state, then "silent → nothing sent · PayPal retries in 4 d"). A gold dot marks S-14, which needs Maya.
2. Layer 1, the desk (inspector): state, retry time, the four levers as one-line rows (an off lever shows a code such as "REPLAY"), and one decision, Review & approve, with its default on silence.
3. ↑/↓ picks the subscriber, 1–4 the lever. Enter opens the approval window; L leaves the renewal to PayPal's retry.
4. Layer 2: ⓘ explains an off lever, REPLAY and R-3. "What it sends ›" opens a sheet with the PayPal calls, rules and template email. "Recovered" and "All ›" open what counts and the audit lines.

## What it does better than the other two
- The deepest view of one subscriber, still on one screen.
- Works entirely from the keyboard.

## Known limits
- Only one subscriber's levers are visible at a time, so there is no comparing.
- Time is text ("in 4 d"), not drawn.
- The approval window is an overlay here; in the app it is its own OS window.
