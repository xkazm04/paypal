# Retry Runway

## The bet
Rescue is a race against PayPal's own retry, so time comes first. Each subscriber is a lane on a Mon–Mon runway: the failure, now, the gold ◆ where PayPal retries and its hatched last 24 h. Maya drags one lever onto one lane; it turns teal if allowed, red if not.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the lever tray (plan price struck) and the runway. A lane carries id · state · "silent → nothing sent", the retry ◆ and the placed lever with Review ↗.
2. Drag a token onto a lane, click token then lane, or focus a lane and press 1–4. S-22 refuses: it sits inside the hatch.
3. Click a lane (or Enter): the inspector holds lever rows, the decision, its default and the simulations.
4. Layer 2: "How to read ⓘ" is the legend; ⓘ explains a refusal; "What it sends ›" opens calls, rules and email. Review hands off to the approval window, then an invoice bar grows on the lane until PAID.

## What it does better than the other two
- Shows why S-22 waits and how much time S-14 has.
- The default on silence is a mark on the timeline, not a sentence.
- A refused drop explains itself on the lane.

## Known limits
- The 8-day axis cuts off a PAUSE that runs to 29 Nov.
- Below 1180 px with the inspector open, the lane's Review hides; the inspector carries it.
