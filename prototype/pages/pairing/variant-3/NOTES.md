# Keyring

## The bet
Make the identity dominant and turn the compare into active recall. The sidebar is Maya's keyring. A new pairing is a card filling in one fact per row. In the approval window Maya picks each word Dan reads from three options her wallet offers, or "none".

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: choose a door, one row each: create a code, join with Dan's code, or the house seller.
2. Watch the card fill in; "Key history" says whether this key is already pinned as Dan. The footer holds the lapse meter, "silent →" line and two buttons.
3. In the approval window pick each word (keys 1-3, 0 for none); a wrong pick means abort. Name the key (a clashing name shows a one-line warning), then "Words match: pin key", idle-locked.
4. Layer 2: the inspector ("Details…") holds the full fingerprint, how it was paired, expiry rules and what the pin does and does not do. Popovers explain the doors, pick, key history and clash.

## What it does better than the other two
It catches a relay three ways: Dan's word is missing from the options, the key was never pinned, and the name clashes. A random guess passes 1 time in 81.

## Known limits
- Key history and the name clash are UX proposals; the built wallet shows only the words.
- The decoy words are page constants.
- FIX has no payee for pixel-bay or ridge-lane.
