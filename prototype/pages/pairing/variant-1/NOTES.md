# Two desks

## The bet
Put both ends on screen. Maya's main window and Dan's simulated screen sit side by side, so the compare reads like a call: four words here, the same four in the same row there. This is the built flow, sharpened: three tabs in, the approval window decides with "Words match: pin key" or "They differ: abort".

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: pick a tab (create, join with TBL-7Q4M-K2, or the house seller). The step is one grouped box: the code with Copy/Cancel, or the four word tiles and two buttons, plus a "silent →" line.
2. The approval window opens over Maya's desk only, so Dan's words stay visible beside it: four big words, a name field, the two buttons, the lapse line. Pin is idle-locked; abort is not.
3. Layer 2: how it works, why the words, expiry rules, the HOUSE explanation, the fingerprint and the idle lock are popovers. The after-pin detail and the relay-in-the-middle story are sheets.
4. "Open a table" unlocks when Dan confirms.

## What it does better than the other two
Most familiar, fastest to learn, smallest change from the React build. Both wallets are visible at once, word row against word row.

## Known limits
- The compare is passive: a hurried owner can click "match" without reading.
- The full fingerprint is a page constant behind FIX's `47be…0d`.
- Unpinning is not shown. Dan's screen is simulated.
