# Sunday Notebook

## The bet
Book is a conversation with the ledger. Each question becomes a notebook entry: Maya's words, then the book agent's query, then the answer, which appears only after she presses Run. The inspector keeps the standing answers: money by state, whether PayPal agrees, and the cash-flow outlook.

## How a person uses it
1. Pick a preset (or press 1–7, no engine needed), or type a question and press Enter.
2. Layer 1 of an entry: the question, the verbatim query on one line with Run read-only beside it, then one line per answer group (money kept apart by state, market percentile, statement chips).
3. Layer 2: "Read" opens the plain-words reading, "Why rejected" the schema errors, "Rows" the rows (each opens its evidence and quarantined note), "Against the market" the band strip.
4. Each outlook row in the inspector says "if you do nothing"; a click or ⓘ opens the detail.
5. Older entries fold to one line; Esc folds the newest. Each answer has its own Export CSV.

## What it does better than the other two
It teaches reading before running. Every answer keeps the query that produced it, so a week of questions becomes an audit-ready notebook. It is the calmest page for a non-technical owner.

## Known limits
- No whole-week grid: rows appear only through a question.
- Entries are lost on reload.
- The engine is simulated; the snapshot is Thu 29 Oct 14:02.
