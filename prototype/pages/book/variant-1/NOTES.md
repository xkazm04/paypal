# Ledger Lens

## The bet
The week's ledger is the page. Every deal sits in one grid whose four money columns (captured, held at PayPal, in motion, stopped) carry their own totals and are never added together. Every row has a PayPal-statement chip. A question doesn't replace the grid: running it lays a lens over the same rows.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: the outlook strip (three items, each with "if you do nothing"), the ask field, and one 28px grid row per deal: id, item, state, amount in its money column, statement.
2. Pick a lens in the sidebar (no engine) or ask in words. The slip shows the verbatim query on one line next to Run read-only (Ctrl+Enter).
3. Running dims the rows the query skipped and adds one pivot line per group. Esc clears the lens.
4. Layer 2: "Read" opens the plain-words reading, "Why rejected" the schema errors, a row its evidence sheet (PayPal ids, decided by, audit, market band, quarantined note), an outlook item its deadline.
5. Filter by a total or by statement; Export CSV keeps each row's mode.

## What it does better than the other two
Without asking, Maya sees totals, reconciliation and all 16 rows on one screen, and checks any query against the full ledger.

## Known limits
- At 1024 item names clip and the market column hides.
- The engine is simulated by keyword rules; the snapshot is Thu 29 Oct 14:02.
