# The Line

## The bet
Words, not objects. At rest the Tumbler is one sentence holding the decision and its default ("If you do nothing: the offer lapses at 18:00 · no money moves"), so Maya never decodes a ring. Ctrl+Shift+Space summons a keyboard-first command card; Esc hands the keyboard back.

## How a person uses it
1. **Layer 1, the line.** The most urgent GATE; a countdown at ≤ 2 h, gold at ≤ 15 min. Arrivals print as lines above it.
2. **Layer 1, the card.** One row per item: id · who · amount · state · countdown, with "silent → default" under it. The selected row adds one action line: Review ↗ (Enter), Withdraw (W twice), Snooze 30 (S), Table (T).
3. **Layer 2, on demand.** I or ⓘ opens a popover: clause, item, deadline, mode. Review opens the approval window: checks and the one money button.
4. / filters. > lists commands (pause, do not disturb, put away); there is no approve command.
5. Esc closes the popover, then the card; focus returns to the spreadsheet cell.

## What it does better than the other two
Triage needs no mouse, and the default on silence is readable at rest. "Today" merges new events with the audit log: what happened while she was away.

## Known limits
- Text must be read; urgency across items reads less well than in the Ribbon.
- Letter keys act only while the list has focus.
- Proposed forms: `line` 560×48, `log1` 560×86, `log2` 560×124, `command` 600×520.
- The approval window is a stand-in.
