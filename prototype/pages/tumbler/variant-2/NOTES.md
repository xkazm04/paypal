# The Ribbon

## The bet
Time comes first. Each open decision is a gold mark at its deadline on one shared log-scale clock, from now to 3 days. "How loudly" means "how close to the left edge". Maya sees the shape of her afternoon, not a count. It sits above the taskbar and flips in place to one sentence when something arrives.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1, ribbon: marks inside 2 h breathe; inside 15 min the top edge turns gold. Hover a mark for its countdown.
2. Layer 1, drawer (click a mark or Ctrl+Shift+Space): one line per item (id, who, state, amount, countdown, default on silence), then the two meters.
3. The bottom bar acts on the selected row: Review ↗, Withdraw (confirms in place), Snooze 30, Open in Table.
4. Layer 2: "ⓘ Details" pops over rule, rung and default; Review opens the approval window, the only yes.
5. Keys: arrows choose, Enter reviews, W twice withdraws, S snoozes, T opens The Table, Esc returns the keyboard.

## What it does better than the other two
It shows each item's urgency next to the others without opening anything. HOLDs sit in a no-clock bin. A STOP flips the ribbon once, with no motion.

## Known limits
- Resting footprint is 680×40, larger than the puck; it folds to a 300×40 stub.
- The log scale shows order, not exact time.
- Proposed forms: `stub` 300×40, `ribbon` 680×40, `drawer` 680×460.
- Reserving the screen edge is UNVERIFIED. The approval window is a stand-in.
