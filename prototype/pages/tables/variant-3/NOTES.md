# Price Ladder

## The bet
One table at a time on a vertical price ladder: your bids climb the left, their asks come down the right, the price on the table sits in the middle, and the band is a fence across it. Moving the band re-signs the mandate, so it goes through three steps in the inspector: draft, what changes, sign.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: two compact table tabs (keys 1, 2) and the ladder. The inspector shows "Needs you" (countersign, offer lapses at 18:00 if silent) and the three steps.
2. Drag the fence (or ↑ ↓ on its grip), or use the fields of step 1.
3. Step 2 shows one line ("3 conflicts · 2 other effects"). Opening it is Layer 2: a sheet with every consequence, where "Looks right · next" unlocks step 3.
4. "Sign v4 in the approval window" hands off. Closed, ⓘ (reading the ladder), note, Market and the countersign ⓘ are popovers.

## What it does better than the other two
It spells out every consequence before anything leaves for the approval window, without putting them on the page. With price as the only axis, the gap between bid, ask and fence is distance on the ladder.

## Known limits
- One table at a time; closed tables sit behind a popover.
- Time order shows only as sequence numbers.
- Signing takes one extra step.
- Chips crowd within about $4.
