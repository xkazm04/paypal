# Band Box

## The bet
The sharpened baseline: tables on the left; on the right the selected table's convergence chart, with the band as a box you drag (ceiling × rounds) and a deadline track. It bets that seeing every signed bid and ask inside the box is the quickest way to judge how close a table is, so the band belongs on that picture.

## How a person uses it
1. Layer 1: a "Needs you" strip (countersign $329, offer lapses at 18:00 if silent), live rows with ask, room and a sparkline, closed rows with price.
2. Select a live row. Drag the ceiling pill, the rounds pill or the deadline handle; arrow keys work too.
3. A one-line draft bar appears. "Sign v4" hands off to the approval window; Esc discards.
4. Layer 2 on demand: "What changes" (diff and exact words), the clause 4 chip (checks), "note" (untrusted title), ⓘ (why clause 6), Market, closed-row sheets, the bands popover.

## What it does better than the other two
It shows history and limits together: bids rising $290→$327, asks falling $389→$329, and the room the box leaves in price and rounds. The countersign strip warns "draft blocks it" when a draft would make $329 unsignable.

## Known limits
- One table's band at a time.
- The rounds axis is fixed at 10.
- At 1024 px the chart drops below the list.
- Approval window and deal page are hand-offs (toast plus prototype buttons).
