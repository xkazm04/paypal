# What-if replay

## The bet
Consequences first, terms second. The window opens on "What would my agents have done this week?": a scoreboard and every intent replayed under v3 and under the draft. The levers sit beside it in the inspector, each carrying the week's deals as ticks. Dragging the per-deal max past $45 turns D-0186 red on the track and in the table at once.

## How a person uses it
1. Read the scoreboard and the replay (Layer 1, one row per intent).
2. Drag a lever, use the arrow keys or type. The counts move ("1 → 4 refused"), changed rows turn gold.
3. Select a row for its reasons in a popover (Layer 2). "In plain words ›" explains each change. Set-type terms open as popovers.
4. Hover "Revoke v3" to preview it in the replay. One click revokes at once.
5. Press "Review & sign v4…" and unlock with Windows Hello (simulated). The sheet holds the diff, consequences, commitment and bytes.

## What it does better than the other two
The only variant where you see the consequence while choosing the number, with no scrolling between. Best for calibrating a threshold.

## Known limits
- The replay covers only this week's ten intents.
- The window (1040 px) is wider than a usual approval window.
- It uses a prototype hash.
- Revoke passing the idle lock needs an owner decision.
