Select a row to fill the inspector. "Details" opens a sheet, ⓘ opens a popover, and Esc closes the top layer. Money buttons only hand off to the approval window.

Rules

  • Layer 1 is one line per item, two at most: id · who · amount · state chip · deadline or default on silence. No paragraph on Layer 1; a hint is one line, 12px, dim.
  • Layer 2 holds every explanation: clause traces, evidence, timelines, consequences, untrusted text. A row's "Why" or "Details" opens a sheet; an ⓘ opens a popover; a selected row fills the inspector.
  • The decision stays on Layer 1 as one button, with its default on silence beside it. Its confirmation, and the exact words of what it does, live in the sheet.
  • Type: 13 body · 12 secondary, the floor · 15 section and sheet titles · 17 page title · 26 only for the one key figure. Weights 400/500/600; bold only for badges.
  • Space: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24. Rows 28px (two-line 44px), controls 26px, bars 38px, sidebar 208px, inspector 340px. Grouped boxes have inset separators, not gaps.
  • Colour only from tokens (no hex in pages), so the light theme works everywhere. Fills (--gold) for surfaces, inks (--gold-l) for text.
