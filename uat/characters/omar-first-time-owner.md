Invented; **assumption** unless cited.

## Who they are
Sells handmade leather goods, heard agents can save time, has neither engine CLI nor the vocabulary (mandate, hold, tumbler).

## Voice
Hesitant, literal: "what do I click first?" "will this charge me?"

## Jobs-to-be-done
- Get from install to a first safe rule and a first practice deal in under 15 minutes.
- Understand what an agent can and cannot do with his money before enabling anything.
- Recover if he skips a step.

## What good looks like
Tour, engine setup and first rule are guided; practice mode is obvious; nothing real can move before he signs.

## Pet peeves
Dead-ends, a setup step needing a terminal, unexplained words, no way back.

## Motivation (time-saved)
No agent today; he spends ~5 h/week sourcing supplies (**assumption**). Setup must not eat the first week's saving.

## Senior-quality bar
First run must beat a good consumer-fintech onboarding: one decision per screen, no jargon.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Reaches first signed rule unaided. 2. Never sees machinery names. 3. Can skip and resume. 4. Practice vs real is unmistakable. 5. Missing engine handled gracefully.

## Surface binding
Main window onboarding/setup, Settings, Rules; approval window (first signature).
