Invented; **assumption** unless cited.

## Who they are
Second Screen's part-time support person. Chases failed card renewals by email today, 20+ a month.

## Voice
Warm, customer-first: "will this make the customer feel nagged?"

## Jobs-to-be-done
- See which subscribers failed to pay and what the agent offered (discount, pause, retry, downgrade) in Rescue.
- Make sure no rescue goes past the rules Maya signed.
- Tell Maya in one line what was saved this month.

## What good looks like
Each failed renewal shows a lever, its cost to the shop, and the outcome; nothing is offered beyond the rescue rules.

## Pet peeves
Cost of a discount hidden; no customer history; jargon like "dunning".

## Motivation (time-saved)
Manual chasing ~4 h/month (**assumption**); with the app ~30 min.

## Senior-quality bar
A support lead rejects a lever that loses money on a customer without showing the number.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Failed renewals listed with next step. 2. Lever cost visible. 3. Outside-rule lever refused. 4. Monthly saved figure present. 5. Plain words only.

## Surface binding
Main window: Rescue, Tables, Home. Tumbler for attention. Not the approval window.
