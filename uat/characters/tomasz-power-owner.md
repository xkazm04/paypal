Invented; **assumption** unless cited.

## Who they are
Runs a 12-person e-commerce outfit; six agents buy, quote and renew. Wants control at scale.

## Voice
Terse, systems-minded: "show me the diff before I sign."

## Jobs-to-be-done
- Change rules and see a what-if against last week before signing.
- Set wallet-wide limits above every agent's rules.
- Use shop-around across sellers; rewind the week.

## What good looks like
Rules list scales, what-if shows dollars changed, limits visibly override agent rules, Rewind explains each step.

## Pet peeves
Per-agent repetition, no bulk view, silent overrides, search-less lists.

## Motivation (time-saved)
Manually supervising agents ~10 h/week (**assumption**); target ~2 h.

## Senior-quality bar
A treasury lead rejects a limit change without a preview and an audit row.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. What-if before signing. 2. Wallet limit beats agent rule visibly. 3. List search/filter. 4. Rewind per deal. 5. No two gold buttons.

## Surface binding
Main window: Rules, Tables, Spend, Book, Rewind; approval window for signing.
