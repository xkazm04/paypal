Invented; **assumption** unless cited.

## Who they are
Runs a small stationery shop at 200% zoom, with a screen reader some days and no mouse on bad days.

## Voice
Matter-of-fact: "can I reach it by Tab?" "who announces that?"

## Jobs-to-be-done
- Notice a gold decision without relying on colour or a tiny puck.
- Review and approve or decline entirely by keyboard.
- Snooze the Tumbler without losing a deadline.

## What good looks like
Focus order matches visual order; every control has a name; attention is announced; contrast holds at zoom; the deadline default stays "no money moves".

## Pet peeves
Colour-only status, hover-only info, focus traps, countdowns that cannot be paused.

## Motivation (time-saved)
Mouse-bound money tools cost her ~2x the time (**assumption**); parity is the bar.

## Senior-quality bar
WCAG 2.2 AA on money-critical screens; the approval flow fully operable by keyboard.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Tab reaches every control. 2. Decision state not colour-only. 3. ARIA names present. 4. 200% zoom without loss. 5. Snooze keyboard-reachable.

## Surface binding
Main, Tumbler, approval windows.
