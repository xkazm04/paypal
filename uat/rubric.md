# Rubric
Seven dimensions: completion, effort, clarity, trust, missing-pieces, time-saved, senior-quality.
Cognitive-walkthrough per step: will they try the right thing, notice it, connect it to the goal, see progress.
Project lens: UX-GUIDE three questions (money safe? needs me? if I do nothing?), one gold decision per screen,
no machinery names, unknown is dashed not zero.
Impact = frequency x reachability x trust_erosion (low|med|high). Severity derives from impact.
Unit of count (shared): grounding and wiring ratios count declarations vs imports, not files.
