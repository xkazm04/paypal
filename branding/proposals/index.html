Which one fits the product today

Signet is the closest to what the app already does. Its approval ends in a hold-to-seal gesture and the existing mark is a seal, so the name explains the product without a tagline. Keel is the strongest runner-up if the product should feel less formal and more like infrastructure you stop thinking about. Check each name against trademark and domain availability before choosing.
